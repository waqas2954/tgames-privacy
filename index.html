Privacy Policy

Catch the Falling Objects · Last updated: October 2026

TGames respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Catch the Falling Objects mobile game.

Information We Collect

Catch the Falling Objects does not directly collect, store, or transmit personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, physical address, or other personally identifiable information.

The game does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play.

Game Data

Game information such as your score or game progress may be stored locally on your device to provide game functionality.

This locally stored game data is not transmitted to or stored by TGames on external servers.

Internet Connection

The current version of Catch the Falling Objects does not require an internet connection or personal information to play its core gameplay.

Advertising and Analytics

The current version of Catch the Falling Objects does not intentionally use third-party advertising or analytics services to collect information about users.

Third-Party Services

The current version of Catch the Falling Objects does not intentionally use third-party services to collect or share users' personal information.

If third-party services are added in a future version and their data practices change, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

Catch the Falling Objects does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Because the game does not require users to provide personal information, TGames does not intentionally collect personal information from children through the game.

Data Security

TGames does not directly collect or maintain personal information from users of Catch the Falling Objects.

Game data that is stored locally on the user's device remains on that device and is not transmitted to TGames.

Data Deletion

Because TGames does not collect or maintain personal information through Catch the Falling Objects, there is no personal information stored by TGames that users need to request deletion of.

Locally stored game data can be removed by uninstalling the game or clearing the game's storage through the device's Android settings.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the game's features or data practices change.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated Last updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Catch the Falling Objects, please contact TGames through the contact information provided on the game's Google Play Store listing.
